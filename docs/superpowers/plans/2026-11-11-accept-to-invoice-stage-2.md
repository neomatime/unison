# Accept to invoice, Stage 2: automatic sending — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Behind its own off-by-default switch, UNISON issues and emails each later month's invoice of a billing plan on its date, without a signed-in user, after the same checks the first invoice passes. A held or failed month waits for a person and tells them, in the app and by email. The first-invoice-ready notices also go by email.

**Architecture:** A daily request to a protected route (`/api/billing/run`, authenticated with the host's cron secret) runs one orchestrator. The orchestrator asks the database which plan items are due (`billing_due_items`), has the database check, lock and issue each one (`billing_run_item`, service role only), then does the part that needs Node: it loads the invoice, makes the PDF, sends the email and records the send. Every decision (which month, which checks, which state) is made in definer functions with row locks and the Stage 1 unique keys, so re-running or running twice creates nothing twice. Exactly one new file may import the service-role client.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 16.3.4 (route handler), Supabase Postgres (definer functions granted to `service_role`), Microsoft Graph mail through the existing `sendEmail`, `node --test` with `--experimental-strip-types`, pnpm, the host's cron (`vercel.json`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-26-accept-to-invoice-to-payment-design.md` (§4 checks, §5 monthly invoices and the runner, §9 stage 2, and "Calls settled after a builder check" 1, 2, 8). Stage 1 (plan, draft first invoice, approval) is built; its plan is `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-26-accept-to-invoice-stage-1.md` and its notes are in `docs/follow-ups.md` under "From accept-to-invoice, Stage 1". Stages 3 and 4 (reminders, starting the work) are not built here.

Every fixture row, audit rows included, is deleted and verified gone (Task 8 has the check). - **The real organisation is never switched on and its data is never touched.** `billing_auto_send_enabled` defaults to false and stays false there. No test, script or walk-through writes to the real organisation's rows. The walk-through uses a throwaway organisation.
- **No real email is sent by any test or walk-through.** The orchestrator is tested with injected fakes; the dev server runs with `EMAIL_TRANSPORT=log`. The only real send is the owner's, after switching on.
- **Switch off means identical.** With `billing_auto_send_enabled` false (the default) nothing new runs: the runner finds no items, sends no notice email, and every Stage 1 behaviour, `issue_invoice`, `record_document_email` and the emailing action behave exactly as before. The refactors in Task 2 change no behaviour.
- **One service-role file.** `features/billing-runner/run-billing.ts` is the only new file that imports `lib/supabase/admin`; it is added to `SERVICE_ROLE_REQUEST_PATHS` in `tests/unit/service-role-boundary.test.ts` (both lists) with its reason. - **Money is never typed or recomputed.** A monthly invoice copies the quote's monthly lines (Stage 1's `build_plan_item_invoice`); totals and VAT come from the existing triggers. - **Git:** stage by explicit path only (never `git add -A`, `git checkout .`, `git restore .`, `git stash`, `git clean`); the working tree holds unrelated uncommitted brand files that stay untouched. ## Review Focus

Failure modes the spec implies that no feature task would otherwise test, most likely first. Each has a test in the task named.

1. **Two runners, or a re-run, never bill a month twice or send an invoice twice.** `Promise.all` on `billing_run_item` and on `billing_send_context`; a second full run after a first creates and sends nothing (Tasks 3, 4, 5).
2. **A month never goes before the first invoice is approved and emailed,** and never while the plan is paused or ended, the item cancelled, or either switch off (Task 3).
3. **A failing check never numbers an invoice and never repeats its notice daily:** the draft is kept, the owner is told once per distinct failure, the next run recovers when the data is fixed (Task 3).
4. **An issued invoice whose email fails is retried, never re-issued, never sent twice after success,** gives up after five attempts, and tells the owner once (Tasks 4, 5).
5. **Midnight is South African.** A run at 22:30 UTC, already the next day in South Africa, bills that day's items; a run at 21:30 UTC does not (Task 3).
6. **The route is closed without the secret:** a missing, empty or wrong secret, or a secret that is not configured, does nothing and says nothing about why (Task 5).

- `public.billing_due_items(p_limit integer) returns table (item_id uuid, step text)`: `step = 'issue'` for each open `monthly` item, `bill_on <= the South African today`, of an `active` plan, in an organisation with both `billing_plans_enabled` and `billing_auto_send_enabled`, whose plan's item 1 has `approved_at` set **and** its invoice has a `document_emails` row, whose `attention_code` is not `CATCH_UP`, and whose linked invoice is absent or an unarchived Draft; `step = 'send'` for each item with `system_issued_at` set, an `Issued` invoice with no `document_emails` row, `send_attempts < 5`, `send_lease_until` null or past, in an organisation with both switches on (the plan's status does not matter: an invoice that was issued goes out). Takes `select ... for update skip locked` on the item (not obtainable → `skipped`), then re-checks every condition of the `issue` step under the lock (any fails → `skipped`, nothing written). Then, in order: the organisation's `document_emails` in the last hour at or above `p_email_cap` → `capped` (nothing written, no notice); **catch-up:** when the plan has more than one open monthly item with `bill_on <=` today and no issued invoice, build this item's draft if it has none (`build_plan_item_invoice`), set `attention_code = 'CATCH_UP'`, write `billing.item_held` on the quote (`Month <n> held for your check: more than one month is due`), notify `billing_notice_users` once for the plan (only from the item with the lowest month number among those) and return `held`; otherwise build the draft if the item has none; `billing_invoice_blockers` non-empty → `attention_code = 'CHECKS_FAILED'`, `attention_detail` the codes joined by commas, `billing.item_held` on the quote (`Month <n> held: <codes>`), a notice only when `attention_notified_at` is null or `attention_detail` changed (then `attention_notified_at = now()`), return `held` with the codes, issuing nothing and consuming no number; otherwise `private.issue_invoice_apply`, set `system_issued_at = now()`, clear the attention columns, write `billing.invoice_auto_issued` on the quote (`Invoice <number> issued automatically for month <n>`), and when the invoice's `vat_rate` differs from the quote's notify `billing_notice_users` (`The VAT rate on <number> is <x>%, not the <y>% on quotation <q>.`), return `issued` with the invoice id. Notices are `notifications` rows (category `Finance`, href `/commercial/quotes/<id>`, titles `Month <n> for <client> needs your attention` / `Month <n> for <client> was issued at a different VAT rate`, bodies without client contact data) written in a block of their own whose failure only warns (SQLSTATE, no text), so a notice that cannot be written never undoes an issue.

## Calls made in this plan (for review)

Where the design is silent or leaves it to the builder. Say if any should change.

1. **The runner** is the host's daily cron calling `/api/billing/run` with `CRON_SECRET` as a bearer token (the convention Vercel Cron uses), at 03:00 UTC (05:00 in South Africa), after the 00:20 database job. The spec's "one new secret" is this one.
2. **The Stage 2 switch** is `organization_settings.billing_auto_send_enabled`, beside the Stage 1 switch, owner/admin only, and it cannot be switched on while billing plans are off. It also gates the notice emails.
3. **A month goes out only when** its plan is active, item 1 is approved **and** has a recorded email (approved but not emailed holds every later month), the month's date has come, its checks pass, the hourly cap has room and nothing else is due in the same plan.
4. **Catch-up:** when more than one month of a plan is due at once, all are kept as drafts marked `CATCH_UP`, one notice is sent, and they stay held until a person issues them with the ordinary Issue button (the spec's "simplest is to hold them"). Releasing one never releases the others.
5. **A failed check** keeps the draft, marks the month `CHECKS_FAILED`, tells the owner once per distinct set of failures (not daily) and retries on each run, so fixing the data is enough.
6. **An issued invoice whose email fails** is retried on later runs, up to five attempts, with a ten-minute lease so two runs never send at once; the owner is told on the first failure and on giving up. After success nothing is resent. If the send succeeds and the record fails, the invoice is not sent again by the runner.
7. **Only invoices the system issued** are emailed by the runner (`system_issued_at`); a monthly draft the owner issues by hand is the owner's to email.
8. **A plan paused after an invoice was issued** still lets that invoice go out; pausing stops issuing, not delivering. Switching either organisation switch off stops everything automatic.
9. **The monthly email is the plain invoice email, not the Welcome Email;** wording is drafted in `billing-run-copy.ts` for the owner's approval. It names the month ("Month 3 of 12"), the total, the due date, the bank details and the payment reference.
10. **Reply-To is the quote's owner,** and the sign-off is the owner's name, else the company name.
11. **VAT:** the rate on the day of issue; a difference from the quote's rate is notified, not blocked (spec call 8; the accountant should confirm).
12. **Notice emails** use a small outbox sent by the same runner, only for organisations with the Stage 2 switch on; pause notices stay in-app.
13. **Log mode** (`EMAIL_TRANSPORT=log`) records nothing and leaves the month to be tried again, so a developer machine never marks an invoice emailed.
14. **At most 20 items are handled per run,** oldest first, so a backlog clears over a few days rather than in one burst; the hourly cap is checked per organisation inside each item.
