# Accept to invoice, Stage 1: the foundation — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** When a client accepts a quote on the portal, UNISON (behind an off-by-default switch) marks the Sales record Won, builds a billing plan for the quote, prepares a draft first invoice and tells the owner; an owner or admin presses **Approve and send** to issue and email it. Quotes carry a start date and term. Nothing can invoice the same month, or the same quote, twice.

**Architecture:** Two new tables (`billing_plans`, `billing_plan_items`) with unique keys, and a unique link from an invoice to its plan item. All writes go through definer functions; `private.billing_on_acceptance` is called from the existing guarded follow-up block of `portal_decide` (via `private.decision_follow_ups`) and from a nightly sweep. The first invoice can only be issued through `approve_first_invoice` (owner/admin); the Node side then emails it with the existing `emailDocumentAction`. Plan state, pause rules and the per-month "Create invoice" action are database functions with thin server actions on the quote page.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 16.3.4 (App Router, server actions), Supabase Postgres (definer functions, pg_cron), `node --test` with `--experimental-strip-types`, pnpm.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-26-accept-to-invoice-to-payment-design.md` (approved 2026-10-26; this plan is its "Stage 1: Foundation", §9, with the calls under "Calls settled after a builder check"). Stages 2 to 4 (automatic sending, reminders, starting the work) are separate plans and are **not** built here. The quote portal (slices 1 to 3) is built and merged.

- **The real organisation is never switched on and its data is never touched.** `billing_plans_enabled` defaults to false and stays false there. No test, script or walk-through writes to the real organisation's rows.
- **No real email is sent.** Tests never call an email action. The walk-through runs the dev server with `EMAIL_TRANSPORT=log`.
- **Switch off means identical.** With `billing_plans_enabled` false (the default): `send_quote`, `create_invoice_from_quote` (apart from the duplicate guard in Task 2), `portal_decide`, `portal_get_quote`, the snapshot hash and the HIVE feed behave exactly as today. `portal_decide` and `get_clients_for_api_key` are **not edited**; only `private.decision_follow_ups` gains one guarded block.

## Review Focus

Failure modes the spec implies that no feature task would otherwise test, most likely first. Each has a test in the task named.

1. **Two clicks, one invoice.** Two simultaneous "Create invoice", "Create this month's invoice", "Approve and send", acceptances, or an acceptance racing the nightly sweep produce one plan, one invoice per item, one approval (Tasks 2, 3, 5; `Promise.all` tests).
2. **A first invoice never leaves without approval.** The ordinary Issue button, a member, and a paused plan all fail; nothing is numbered when a check fails (Task 3).
3. **Month dates do not drift.** A start on the 29th to 31st, a term of 12 and 24, a leap year: each month is `start + (n−1) months`, never chained (Task 2).
4. **A failed billing step never undoes an acceptance,** and the sweep recovers it; an acceptance from before the switch was turned on is never billed (Task 5).
5. **The quote the client saw is the quote that is billed.** A quote with a monthly line cannot be sent (switch on) without a start date, a term and a charge kind on every line; terms are frozen after sending; a snapshot without terms is byte-identical to today's (Tasks 1, 6, 7).

## Calls made in this plan (for review)

Where the design is silent or leaves it to the builder. Say if any should change.

1. **The Stage 1 switch** is `organization_settings.billing_plans_enabled`, on the Company details page beside the portal switch, owner/admin only. `billing_plans_enabled_at` is stamped when it is turned on so the nightly sweep never bills an acceptance from before that moment.
2. **Switching it off stops** creating plans, creating plan invoices and approving; existing plans stay visible on their quotes.
3. **Owner/admin** is the existing `company.manage` permission in the app and `has_role(owner, admin)` in the database; any member can press "Create invoice" on a due item.
4. **In-app notice only** in Stage 1 (the email notice needs Stage 2's system email path); the portal's existing email to the owner about the acceptance is unchanged.
5. **Approve and send emails with the approver's session** (Reply-To is the approver); the quote owner as Reply-To comes with Stage 2.
6. **The first invoice's due date is left blank** on the draft; `issue_invoice` then sets issue day plus the company's payment days, and the owner may type a date.
7. **Every line needs a charge kind** when a quote has a monthly line while the switch is on, so the portal's grouped view and the plan can never disagree.
8. **The existing "Create invoice" button** allows one live invoice per quote (live = not cancelled, and not an archived draft); with a plan it points to the plan; with the switch on and terms on the quote it builds the plan and item 1 instead.
9. **A monthly item can be invoiced by hand only once its date has arrived;** the draft is issued with the normal Issue button, the first invoice only through approval.
10. **An archived draft frees its item; a cancelled issued invoice does not** (the month stays billed; a replacement is raised by hand).
11. **Sales:** probability and other fields are untouched; a Lost opportunity becomes Won with its lost reason cleared; the value rule is the design's decision 12.
12. **Resuming a paused plan, and ending one, are manual** and need an owner or admin; ending cancels only items without an invoice.
13. **Bank details are "complete"** when bank name, account name, account number and branch code are set (account type is optional).
14. **The term is 1 to 120 months;** the start date may be any real date from 2020 to 2100.
15. **Item 1's `bill_on` is the acceptance day** (informational; the first invoice is released by approval, not by date).
16. **The Invoices list is not changed;** the invoice page carries the "waiting for approval" banner instead. The onboarding record and task of the design's §2 are Stage 4.
17. **Filling terms and offerings stays available on a plan draft.** The design locks the amounts (lines, prices, quantities) and the header fields, not the wording: `set_document_terms` and `set_document_offerings` (definer functions, draft-only) may still write `payment_terms`, `terms_sources` and the offerings of a plan draft. They carry no money, and the payment terms text falls back to the company default at issue.
